# v0.8 motion analysis

User authorization: implement the previously discussed gait features, readable personal trends, install directly on the connected phone. Continue in this checkout. No detector tuning, new backend, new dependency, or biometric accuracy claim.

Architecture: pure motion classes appended to lib/gait_engine.dart; acquisition and presentation in lib/main.dart; nested derived exports in lib/session_recorder.dart. Existing raw stream headers, seven files and original detector remain unchanged. Derived motion bins live in summary.json, with definitions/parameters in metadata.json.

## Behavior

1. Independent one-second time bins show quiet, rhythmic movement, other movement, notable rotation, or unavailable. They are exploratory signal states, not validated walking/running or turn labels. Dynamic acceleration magnitude RMS below 0.2 m/s² and gyro RMS below 0.15 rad/s suggests quiet; rhythmic movement needs periodicity >=0.55 and candidate cadence >0; notable rotation needs absolute mean calibrated vertical angular velocity >=0.35 rad/s. Other movement otherwise. Missing gyro does not prevent acceleration states but cannot establish quiet confidently: use other/unavailable with explicit coverage. Require >=80% valid acceleration coverage. Invalid data are unavailable; calibration failure suppresses body axes but retains whole-vector analysis; no duration credit over acceleration gaps >70 ms. Display a compact duration breakdown and trailing 120s timeline. Do not label fatigue or gait quality.
2. Optional body-direction calibration is disabled by default. Enable only at rear central waist, screen facing body or away (explicit user selection). After Start allow 10 seconds to mount, collect the next 5 seconds standing upright and still. Haptic feedback signals collection and completion when foreground. Reject fewer than 200 samples, gaps >70 ms, gravity magnitude outside 8.5–11 m/s², vector SD >0.2 m/s², screen plane nearly horizontal (horizontal normal magnitude <0.5), or nonfinite input. Calibration fixes up from mean gravity and forward from the horizontal projection of phone +z (negate if screen away). Lateral = up cross forward. Axes represent the assumed mounting arrangement, not independent anatomical validation. Restrict other placements. No silent guessed axes.
3. Dynamic acceleration subtracts a sensor-time low-pass gravity estimate (tau 0.5s). Five-second warmup suppresses outputs. Store per-bin whole-vector RMS and, after valid calibration, projected AP/ML/vertical RMS and squared-amplitude shares summing to100%. RMS units m/s², not force/displacement. All axes are fixed to calibrated phone/body mounting; movement >35 degrees in low-pass up direction invalidates calibration until next session. It cannot detect yaw-only belt slip. Gaps >70ms reset filter warmup. Data-invalid bins cannot report body axes. Do not reuse old bins after failed calibration.
4. Independent gyro integration uses its own timestamps, no row matching or claimed synchronization. Per-bin RMS angular speed (rad/s) always available with >=80% contiguous gyro coverage, projected vertical mean rate only with calibration valid and overlapping reliable calibration validity. Do not integrate angle. Whole-vector gyro RMS is orientation-invariant and describes phone rotation intensity, not pelvis angle. Gaps >70ms have no time credit. Stale gyro does not carry values forward into missing intervals. Nonfinite/nonincreasing samples rejected independently.
5. Results card before detailed charts: current state, cumulative duration breakdown, whole-vector motion/rotation values, calibrated directional shares when available, calibration progress or failure reason, explicit uncertainty text. Use Wrap/Column and textual values, no color-only meaning. Minimum320px, text1.6x. Existing cadence and comparison remain separate.
6. Exports include complete derived bins, coverage, calibration axes/status/failure, thresholds, time constants, warmup, invalid counts and limitations in nested motion_analysis. Existing metrics.csv and raw schemas unchanged. New optional recorder args preserve all old callers. App version0.8.0+8.

## Acceptance

Analytical synthetic axis rotations and known gyro values test units/energy/coverage; gaps, rate changes, long gaps, invalid values, unavailable calibration, wrong mount and failed calibration are explicit. These are engineering tests, not human validation. Stationary hardware smoke plus screen-off continuity and stop/save check after installation; no reference-based body-motion accuracy claim. Public-data activity labels can support exploratory distribution checks only, not validated classifier status. Update FEATURE_STATUS, METRIC_ASSESSMENT, CHANGELOG and VALIDATION with actual evidence.

## Builder review decisions

Use separate time-integral accumulators and mutable derived bins, clip queued gyro intervals to the acceleration watermark, and project only whole bins inside the calibrated interval. Missing spans are coalesced to keep very long gaps compact. Lateral sign is not reported; only RMS is exposed. Zero directional energy has null percentages. Stopping early finalizes calibration as failed with a reason. Numerical vector magnitude is bounded at1e6 to reject finite overflow inputs; raw exports remain untouched. The previously approved implementation and direct installation scope remains in force.
